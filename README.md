<sub><b><code>05</code> &nbsp;HEALTHCARE · AI VISION</b></sub>

### SurgiVision

**An AI assistant for gallbladder surgery.**

Before the operation it scores the case's risk. During it, a model I trained on real surgery video outlines the liver, gallbladder and instruments live, and simulated smart glasses speak safety checks like *"Is the critical view met before you clip?"* Afterwards it writes up what happened. A research prototype, not for clinical use.

<sub><b><code>06</code> &nbsp;ACCESSIBILITY · ROBOTICS</b></sub>

### [SightSense](https://github.com/ConnorSawaya/SightSense)

**Autonomous walking guidance for people who are blind.**

A self-steering cane that sees the way ahead for you. Say *"take me to the door"* and a motorized wheel at its tip steers you there, around chairs, people and walls. An iPhone's camera and LiDAR feed a local AI that picks a clear path, names objects, reads signs and talks you through it. An experimental prototype.

![1st Place, UCSC AI Hackathon](https://img.shields.io/badge/%F0%9F%A5%87%201st%20Place-UCSC%20AI%20Hackathon-F5B700?style=flat-square&labelColor=1f2328)
